About Me

I'm a student at Brigham Young University who also works as a financial advisor. In that role I help clients plan for their goals, and I train newer advisors by sitting in on their client meetings and coaching them afterward.

Before college I finished high school a year early, earned the rank of Eagle Scout, and served a full-time mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Those experiences taught me to lead, to work hard, and to communicate with people from every walk of life.
