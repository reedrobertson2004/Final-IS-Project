Why I Love Pickleball

Pickleball mixes the fun of ping-pong, the strategy of tennis, and the social side of a neighborhood pickup game. Anyone can learn it in an afternoon.

The Game

Pickleball is played with solid paddles and a plastic ball with holes, on a court about a third the size of a tennis court. Games go to 11, and you have to win by 2.

What hooked me is that it rewards smart placement and patience as much as power. A soft "dink" into the kitchen can win a point just as easily as a hard drive.

Fun fact: the area right next to the net is called the kitchen. You can't volley the ball while standing in it.

Reasons Pickleball Is Amazing

  1. Easy to learn
    • Small court and simple rules
    • You can play a real game on your first day
  2. Great exercise
    • Cardio
    • Agility and footwork
    • Hand-eye coordination
  3. Social and fun
    • Usually played as doubles
    • Courts are popping up in parks everywhere
